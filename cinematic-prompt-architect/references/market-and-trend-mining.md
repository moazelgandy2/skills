# Market & Trend Mining: Live Category Intelligence

Top creative strategists never ideate in a vacuum. Before writing a single hook or scene, the strategist subagent executes an **Autonomous Live Market & Category Research Pass**. This reference establishes the forensic search protocols used to uncover winning competitor patterns, trending social hooks, and authentic Voice of Customer (VoC) sentiment.

---

## 2. Search Vector 1: Competitor Ad Audit & Longevity Heuristic

### The 60-Day Longevity Rule
- **The Axiom**: In performance marketing, brands turn off unprofitable ads within 3–7 days. If an ad has been actively running for **60 to 180+ days** on Meta (Facebook/Instagram), Google, or TikTok, it is almost certainly a **profitable control ad** generating the majority of the brand's revenue.
- **Strategist Heuristic**: Never copy the copy; reverse-engineer the **underlying angle**:
  - What is the first 3 seconds doing? (Shock, curiosity, direct problem callout, sensory demonstration).
  - What is the core mechanism/claim?
  - What is the primary objection addressed?

### Live Search String Templates:
- `"[Product Category]" "ad" site:facebook.com/ads/library OR "Meta Ad Library"`
- `best performing ads "[product category or main competitor]"`
- `"[competitor brand]" ad campaign breakdown hook angle`

## 3. Search Vector 2: Trending Social Formats & High-CTR Pacing

### TikTok Creative Center & Short-Form Video Trends
Examine the top-performing creative mechanics currently commanding high hold rates (percentage of viewers watching past 3 seconds):

1. **The "Green Screen / Commentary" Format**:
   - Creator in lower third reacting to a bizarre article, shocking review, or competitor claim. High trust, low corporate polish.
2. **The "ASMR Unboxing & Tactile Torture Test"**:
   - Ultra-close macro microphone, zero speech for 3 seconds, satisfying mechanical clicks, peelings, or violent drops.
3. **The "Split Screen Chaos Hook"**:
   - Top half: relatable struggle or storytelling; Bottom half: hyper-engaging kinetic visual (hydraulic press, kinetic sand, soap cutting) to force retention through cognitive stimulation.
4. **The "Confessional Low-Angle Bedroom / Car Hook"**:
   - Handheld camera resting on steering wheel or lap; intimate, whispered vocal delivery: *"I wasn't going to post this, but..."*

### Live Search String Templates:
- `tiktok creative center top ads "[product category]"`
- `viral video ad hooks 2026 "[product category]"`
- `best UGC video ad hooks high CTR`

## 4. Search Vector 3: Voice of Customer (VoC) Mining

### Mining Real Human Friction & Unfiltered Language
Corporate copywriters write sanitized prose; real humans speak in raw, visceral, emotional fragments. The subagent mines Reddit, forums, and Amazon reviews to capture exact verbatim phrases.

### The 1-Star vs. 5-Star Review Matrix
- **Read 1-Star & 2-Star Reviews of Competitors**:
  - Find what customers **hate**: "Broke after 2 weeks", "Customer service ghosted me", "Tastes like chalk", "Hidden subscription fee".
  - *Strategic Gold*: This becomes the opening hook or "The Enemy" of your ad.
- **Read 5-Star Reviews of Category Leaders**:
  - Find what gave customers **genuine relief**: "Finally a jacket where my shoulders can breathe", "Saved my marriage from loud snoring", "I cried when I saw the results".
  - *Strategic Gold*: This becomes your Key Consumer Benefit (KCB) and payoff line.

### Live Search String Templates:
- `site:reddit.com "[product category]" "the worst part about" OR "frustrating" OR "waste of money"`
- `site:reddit.com "[product category]" "finally found" OR "game changer" OR "worth it"`
- `"[product category or competitor]" complaints reviews problems`

## 5. Search Vector 4: The Category Trope Audit (What to Kill)

Every industry has lazy creative clichés that audience brains have evolved to automatically filter out:
- **SaaS / Tech**: Stylized 2D corporate flat illustrations, smiling people pointing at laptop screens, generic synth music.
- **Skincare / Cosmetics**: Model in white bathrobe splashing water in slow motion onto their face, dropper dripping oil onto cheekbone.
- **Beverage / Food**: Condensation running down glass, slow-motion lemon slice dropping into liquid, ecstatic smiling chewing face.
- **Fitness / Supplements**: Sweaty muscular athlete sprinting in empty dark gym, shouting trainer.

### The Rule of Creative Inversion:
List the top 3 category clichés uncovered during live research, and **deliberately ban them from the creative brief**. Invert the cliché:
- If all competitors use bright studio lighting $\rightarrow$ Use moody, cinematic chiaroscuro or gritty handheld realism.
- If all competitors feature smiling 25-year-old influencers $\rightarrow$ Feature a deadpan, stoic 60-year-old craftsman or a blunt anti-hero.
- If all competitors use high-energy upbeat music $\rightarrow$ Open with dead acoustic silence and a single tactile Foley hit.
